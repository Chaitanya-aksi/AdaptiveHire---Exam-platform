## Where the new pages live

Both new destinations are **tabs inside the Assessments section**, not new slots
in the top bar — the bar lists sections, and a section with more than one view
carries its own `SubNav` (`section-tabs.ts`). Left to grow, "Reports" and
"Proctoring signals" would have sat beside "Assessments" as though they were
different parts of the product rather than three ways of looking at the same one.

    Assessments → Assessments · Reports · Proctoring signals

## 1 & 2 — dashboard tiles landed one page short

Both tiles named an action ("Host a test", "Build your question bank") and then
delivered a list, leaving the reader to find the button that does the thing.

- `Host a test` → `/admin/assessments/new`, which already existed as its own
  page.
- `Build your question bank` → `/admin/questions/new`. The question editor was
  local state on the bank page (`setEditor({ question: null })`) with no URL of
  its own, so a route was added that mounts the same page with the editor open.
  Cancelling or saving returns to `/admin/questions`. One component, one editor,
  and the form is now linkable and survives a reload.

The hero's "Create an assessment" button had the same problem and was moved with
them.

## 3 — Proctoring signals

Asked for: the list of signals the platform can identify, in preference to one
candidate's events.

`/admin/proctoring` documents all seven event types — what is measured, what it
cannot tell you, and whether it gates anything — and puts this organisation's
own counts beside each one: how many times it has been recorded, across how many
attempts, and when it was last seen. Reading the catalogue and the evidence in
one place is the point; a page of definitions alone would be a manual.

Deliberate content decisions:

- Every entry states its limits in the same voice the platform uses elsewhere.
  `background_noise` cannot tell a voice from a television. `multiple_displays`
  cannot see two windows on one monitor. An entry that overstated what it
  measured would put a claim in a hiring decision the measurement does not
  support.
- Each row says whether it blocks anything. Nothing here does, once a module is
  running — the two gates (camera, readiness) both sit before the attempt
  starts. This is the philosophy in CLAUDE.md, stated where a recruiter will
  actually read it.
- Counts are scoped to the organisation through the assessment that owns the
  session, and the page links into the attempts carrying each signal.

New endpoint: `GET /proctoring/signals` (recruiter, `@CurrentOrg()`).

## 4 — Candidate reports

Asked for: every candidate report across the workspace, most recent first.

`/admin/reports` lists every submitted attempt in the organisation, newest
first, with the assessment it belongs to, the score, the recommendation and the
signal count, filterable by assessment and searchable by name or email. It is
the cross-assessment view; `/admin/assessments/:id/results` stays as the cohort
view for one test, where ranking and shortlisting live.

New endpoint: `GET /reports/attempts` (recruiter, `@CurrentOrg()`). It does not
rank — a standing only means something within one assessment's cohort, so the
cross-assessment list deliberately carries no rank column.

## 5 — Account menu icons

`◍` and `⤴` were text glyphs standing in for icons; they render at whatever the
system font decides and sit at the wrong optical weight beside the label. They
are now `IconUser` and `IconSignOut` in the existing hand-rolled set, so they
inherit `currentColor` and the same 1.6 stroke as every other icon.

## 6 — Question performance: dashes and 0 attempts

**Not a bug.** Verified against the database:

- 186 MCQ questions in the bank.
- 220 responses over 20 submitted sessions, of which 117 are scored objective
  answers.
- 55 distinct questions have ever been answered in a scored attempt; the busiest
  has **5** attempts.
- `MIN_ATTEMPTS` in `item-analysis.service.ts` is **20**, and every figure on
  that page is a proportion or a correlation — both are noise on five
  observations. A discrimination of −0.4 from five attempts would send somebody
  to retire a perfectly good question.

So every row is correctly reporting that it has nothing to report. What was
wrong is that it did not say so where the reader looks: three large figures
reading "— / — / 0" read as broken data, and the explanation sat in a small chip
labelled "Not enough attempts yet" — which is also the wrong sentence for a
question that has never been asked at all.

Fixed in presentation, with the threshold left alone:

- `ItemAnalysis.attemptsNeeded` is new on the API, so the client can state the
  threshold without hardcoding it. Below it, the three figures are replaced by
  one line — "Never asked" or "1 of 20 attempts", plus "19 more before it is
  scored" — rather than "— / — / 0".
- A question with **no** attempts is separated from one with some. "Not enough
  attempts yet" is the wrong sentence for a question nobody has ever been
  served; it implies some were made.
- A banner at the top says how many questions have enough attempts and what the
  threshold is, so the dashes are explained before they are met. On this bank it
  currently reads *0 of 186*.
- The `insufficient_data` chip is gone from those rows. It was the only flag
  they carried and the figures beside it now say the same thing more precisely;
  expanding the row gives the tailored sentence instead.
- The option pick-rate column is suppressed below the threshold instead of
  drawing a chart of zeroes. The options themselves still show, correct answer
  marked — those are worth reading whatever the statistics say.

**If the page should become useful sooner, the lever is `MIN_ATTEMPTS` in
`item-analysis.service.ts`, and lowering it is a real trade** — every figure
there is a proportion or a correlation, and at five attempts a discrimination of
−0.4 is noise that would send somebody to retire a good question. Left at 20.
